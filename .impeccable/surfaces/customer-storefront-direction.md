# Customer storefront direction contract

Build path: code-led. Mode: persuade. Direction seed `e62ecc65` records the earlier ideation round; the user's explicit visual direction supersedes that round. **Creative direction: premium Pokémon collecting boutique — ivory, black, and measured red, with a restrained editorial voice.** The previous full-pixel UI and nocturnal gallery were rejected. Pokémon pixel art stays in the original Kanto home scene and the separate Pueblo Primavera authentication scene; the large authentication backdrop circle remains removed. The customer storefront remains separate from the admin console. Do not start or stop servers, commit, or push; leave Git changes for the user.

## THESIS

Nevada TCG is a boutique for Pokémon TCG collectors who browse individual cards, sealed products, and accessories. Treat every card image as a considered collectible in a light, editorial catalogue. Ivory gives the merchandise room; near-black makes hierarchy confident; deep red marks the primary action and a small number of selected states. Keep catalog facts legible, comparison easy, and shopping direct.

## OWN-WORLD

**La Vitrina de Coleccionismo.** Warm ivory paper, near-black ink, fine sand-toned dividers, and a disciplined Poké Ball red make a quiet premium shell. A system serif gives headlines an editorial character; Geist Sans keeps Spanish copy, controls, and long product names clear; Geist Mono is limited to prices, stock, SKUs, card numbers, and totals. Real card photography and the retained pixel scenes provide the Pokémon personality. Use generous spacing, restrained depth, crisp product art, and deliberate 12–16px corner radii.

## STORY

Collectors arrive at a succinct promise beside the original Kanto pixel-art scene, then choose the catalog or single cards. They scan real product images with collection, condition, stock, and price visible; examine one card in detail; and add it to a local cart. Checkout preserves delivery and payment selection, backend price and stock validation, and final totals. Authentication, account, orders, points, notifications, and support share the boutique visual language while keeping each task order intact.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

When publishable news exists, lead with a compact photographic banner using the active story's cover image as its background and a dark overlay behind legible copy. Then show the two-column hero: an editorial two-line promise and short description on the left, the existing Kanto pixel-art map with crisp Pokémon sprite on the right. The red “Explorar catálogo” control is the sole strong action; “Cartas sueltas” remains quiet. Do not add a dark or colored circle behind the hero character. Follow the scene with a compact row naming the three product categories. At mobile widths, stack the banner copy and controls cleanly, then stack hero copy and actions ahead of the map, companion choice, and category row without clipping the shared header.

## SIGNATURE INTERACTION

The companion selector changes the displayed pixel sprite and clearly shows the active selection with mouse and keyboard. Product choices and add-to-cart feedback stay direct; gallery presentation must not insert steps into purchase.

## CROSS-SURFACE REACH

Use the ivory, ink, red, and warm-neutral tokens across customer home, catalog and filters, product detail, auth, cart, checkout, account, orders, loyalty, notifications, and support. Keep real merchandise imagery dominant. Preserve the distinct Pueblo Primavera authentication map and keep the large character backdrop circle absent. Typography, surfaces, borders, and status colors follow the boutique system. Do not recolor or blur pixel-art assets. Keep admin styling independent.

## HONEST RISK

Ivory storefronts can lose structure when every surface is equally pale; separate paper, cards, and controls with restrained neutral layering and clear warm-gray borders. Red can signal errors as well as primary intent, so reserve the main shade for the principal shop action and active markers; use semantic colors for outcomes. Keep body text and product data readable against every surface, and don't let a polished frame imply a business guarantee that PRODUCT.md has not confirmed.

## QUALITY BAR

- Active news appears above the hero in a cover-image banner, followed by clear catalog and single-card paths beside the retained Kanto pixel scene.
- Editorial typography, warm ivory, black ink, and measured red read as one restrained boutique across customer routes.
- Pixel rendering belongs to the original maps and character sprites; all type, controls, forms, data, and commerce surfaces remain modern.
- Home and Pueblo Primavera authentication maps stay distinct, and the removed authentication circle stays absent.
- Card art and catalog-backed names, collection, stock, SKU, card numbers, prices, and totals remain legible and factual.
- Desktop, mobile, and 344px widths preserve navigation, headings, filters, product cards, forms, account states, and purchase actions without horizontal overflow. Keep keyboard focus, reduced motion, contrast, loading, empty, error, and disabled states explicit.

## CHALLENGER VERDICTS

- **Art auction room — competitive:** strong provenance and exhibit framing suit rare cards, but auction cues can imply bidding or scarcity the shop does not offer. Keep the deliberate presentation of each real card.
- **Collector archive — competitive:** it makes set and card-number data easy to compare, but too much filing language would flatten the boutique feel. Keep set, condition, and number scannable.
- **Modern apothecary — competitive:** restrained labels and cream stock feel premium, but a medicinal metaphor would obscure the Pokémon assortment. Keep calm packaging and generous paper tones.
- **Nocturnal gallery — declined, loses both axes:** the dark frame conflicts with the user's explicit ivory boutique choice and dulls real card photography. Keep the art-first hierarchy.
- **Full pixel RPG — declined, loses both axes:** pixel controls and type were explicitly rejected; they make long purchase tasks harder to scan. Keep pixel art as the retained illustration.
- **Red urgency marketplace — declined, loses both axes:** all-over red and scarcity devices would undermine the sober tone and imply unverified claims. Keep red disciplined and factual stock visible.
